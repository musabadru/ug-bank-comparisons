Excise duty not included

Almost every figure is exclusive of the 15% URA excise duty; loan charges are generally exempt. A UGX 2,000 EFT really costs about UGX 2,300.

All 8 are regulated

All are supervised by the Bank of Uganda. Deposits are insured up to UGX 10 million per depositor by the Deposit Protection Fund.

Entry account ≠ your account

Figures are for the cheapest entry or pay-as-you-transact account unless stated. Bundled accounts swap per-transaction fees for one flat fee.

👤 Personal banking

Winner · zero-fee everyday

Equity Bank

No monthly fee and no minimum balance on UGX and USD accounts. Free deposits at Equity Duuka agents; ATM withdrawals UGX 1,200.

Avoid branch counter withdrawals (UGX 10,000–30,000).

Winner · salary & convenience

Stanbic Bank

Everyday Banking at UGX 0, or Karibu Salary (6,500) and SMART (8,500) with free ATM use and SMS alerts. Widest ATM network and cheapest EFTs.

Sleeper pick · low hassle

I&M Bank

Paygo (UGX 3,000) with no minimum balance and free insurance. ATM UGX 1,000 own, UGX 2,500 at other local ATMs, against 8,000–14,600 elsewhere.

Only if you're upcountry

Centenary Bank

Best rural branch and agent coverage. Watch per-entry charges and steep online card fees.

💼 Business banking

Winner · established businesses

Stanbic Bank

Business Online, cash-in-transit, POS and trade finance. Corporate current UGX 35,000/month; EFT 2,000, RTGS 10,000.

Winner · SMEs & EA trade

Equity or KCB

Equity: free cash deposits at agents, USD 3–6 cross-border transfers, Corporate IB UGX 150,000/yr with 3,000 EFTs. KCB: Corporate PAYT at UGX 0/month, 3,000 EFTs, 2,000 bulk mobile-money payouts.

Winner · digital & online merchants

Absa Bank

Business Access from UGX 10,000/month. Pay by Link lets clients pay with any Visa card, settled instantly, with no website or POS needed.

Niche picks

NCBA · Exim

NCBA for asset finance and leasing. Exim for heavy Tanzania trade and a responsive FX desk.

🌐 Online banking & international payments

#1 · digital experience

Absa Bank

Smoothest app; open USD, GBP and EUR accounts without a branch visit. Use the Multi-Currency Virtual Card, not the UGX debit card (6% fee).

#2 · USD freelancers

Equity Bank

USD account with no fees and no minimum, free USD cash deposits up to USD 5,000/day, Visa USD Gold with free online spend. SWIFT EQBLUGKAXXX for Wise and Payoneer.

#3 · prepaid for shopping

I&M Bank

Multi-Currency Mastercard Prepaid with 9 currencies (UGX, USD, GBP, EUR, KES, TZS, RWF, ZAR, INR), locked-in FX rates, and online limits from UGX 7M up to 30M.

⚠️ Avoid for online and international card shopping: Centenary Bank

The 2026 tariff guide charges UGX 2,000 for a domestic online card payment and 2.5% + 5% FX mark-up on international e-commerce.

Quick verdict

What should you actually join?

The smartest setup in Uganda is usually one everyday local bank plus one strong digital or FX bank. Both can cost UGX 0 a month.

1

One bank for everything

Personal, online and a side business: Absa (Flexi + Multi-Currency Virtual Card) or Equity (UGX Ordinary + USD account with Visa USD Gold).

2

Salary, heavy transfers, formal company

Stanbic: Everyday or Karibu for personal; Business Online for companies.

3

Low fees, no queues, cheap ATMs

I&M or KCB pay-as-you-transact accounts.

Suggested combo: Stanbic or Equity for everyday local banking, plus Absa or I&M for online and FX.
Fact-check

What we checked against official tariffs

Where a figure in the original summary differed from a published bank document, it's flagged here.

Stanbic ✓The March 2026 Personal & Private Banking guide confirms Everyday Banking is free, plus account fees of 6,500 and 8,500 and a 17,500 bi-annual Classic Visa fee. It also lists a 7% currency-conversion fee.
Equity ✓Equity Bank Uganda confirmed on X that ATM withdrawals cost UGX 1,200 for amounts up to UGX 2 million.
I&M ✓I&M's tariff guide lists UGX 2,500 at non-I&M local ATMs and UGX 10,000 at international ATMs.
Exim ⚠The Exim tariff sheet lists an ATM access fee of UGX 1,000 for Exim cards (2,000 at Interswitch, 7,000 at other Visa ATMs). The original summary said UGX 700, so this page uses 1,000.
KCB ⚠KCB's corporate tariff guide (Nov 2025) lists UGX 3,000 for KCB-to-KCB transfers and UGX 5,000 for EFT to other banks. The UGX 3,000 EFT may apply only to some channels or account types.
NCBA ⚠The newest NCBA Uganda tariff found online is the 2024 edition. Its corporate guide lists outward RTGS at UGX 25,000 at a branch; UGX 10,000 here is the mobile RTGS rate.
Absa ⓘAbsa's retail, business and corporate guides are published on absa.co.ug but weren't reviewed line by line for this page.
