# Uganda Bank Fees 2025–2026: 8 Banks Compared

An interactive, mobile-friendly HTML/CSS infographic comparing tariffs at Stanbic, Equity, Absa, Centenary, KCB, I&M, NCBA and Exim Bank Uganda. It covers monthly fees, ATM withdrawals, EFT/RTGS transfers and online/FX card costs, with filters (by bank, use case and zero-fee accounts), sorting, shareable comparison links, category winners, a fact-check against official tariff guides, and sources.

**Live site:** https://musabadru.github.io/ug-bank-comparisons/

Found an error? Use the "Suggest a correction" form on the page, or email info@musabadru.com.

Fees exclude the 15% URA excise duty unless stated. This isn't financial advice.
